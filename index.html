Remote support

Get help on your screen. You stay in control.

Set up RustDesk once. After that, Jonathan can request a session, and nothing happens until you click Accept.

Open the install page for setup instructions.

Common questions

Can you see my computer when I'm not there?

Not without your approval. RustDesk may keep running in the background so Jonathan can see that your computer is online, but each session needs you to click Accept. You can end it at any time, and you never need to send a password.

What information does Jonathan's device list show?

Your RustDesk ID, computer name, username, operating system, RustDesk version, and when your computer was last online. It doesn't include your files or screen. Uninstalling RustDesk stops these updates.

Does it cost anything?

No. RustDesk is free and open-source, and sessions have no time limit.

Where does the connection go?

Jonathan's own server introduces the two computers. The encrypted session then runs directly between them when possible, or through that same server when a network blocks a direct connection.

What if it doesn't connect?

Run the connection check, then contact Jonathan. Workplace networks sometimes block remote support tools, and a phone hotspot is a quick way to confirm that.
